# Draft: request for case law access

Send to GhaLII (contact form at ghalii.org, or via Laws.Africa, their platform
partner). Adapt the numbers before sending — they should be true on the day you
send it.

Why this is worth doing rather than scraping: GhaLII's robots.txt sets
`ai-input=no`, which their own preamble defines as retrieval augmented
generation, and disallows `/judgments/` to every crawler. Their mission is
widening access to Ghanaian law, which is also what this project is for, so the
request is aligned rather than adversarial.

---

**Subject:** Request for judgment access — free legal information service for Ghanaians

Dear GhaLII team,

I run a free chatbot that answers everyday legal questions for Ghanaians on
WhatsApp and Telegram. It is not a commercial product: there is no subscription,
no advertising and no fee to users. It currently answers from Ghanaian
legislation and directs every user to the Legal Aid Commission for matters that
need a lawyer.

Most of the people using it are asking practical questions — whether a landlord
can evict them, how property passes when someone dies without a will, what
happens after an arrest. Legislation answers part of that. Case law is what shows
how the courts actually apply those rules, and it is the main gap in what I can
offer.

I noticed your robots.txt sets `ai-input=no` and disallows `/judgments/`, so I
have not crawled the site and will not. I would rather ask.

Would you consider granting access to judgment data for this use — whether
through a bulk export, an API key, Pocket Law, or any arrangement you prefer? I
am happy to accept conditions, including:

- attributing GhaLII in every answer that draws on your material
- linking users back to the judgment on ghalii.org
- not redistributing the corpus or making it downloadable
- rate limits, a review of how the material is used, or a trial period

If a licence fee applies I would like to know the terms, though I should be
honest that the service currently has no revenue.

Happy to share the source code, the exact retrieval method, or a demonstration of
the bot if that helps you assess the request.

Thank you for the work you do making Ghanaian law available.

Kind regards,
