# Criminal Offences (Amendment) Act, 2023 (Act 1101) — summary

Curated summary. The full text of this Act is not in the bot's document collection
(the Parliament repository does not hold it), so this note records its effect.
Sources: judy.legal record of Act 1101; Death Penalty Project press release; Citi
Newsroom report of presidential assent, August 2023. Gazette notification:
2 August 2023.

## Death penalty abolished — murder is now punished by life imprisonment

The Criminal Offences (Amendment) Act, 2023 (Act 1101) amended the Criminal
Offences Act, 1960 (Act 29) to remove the death penalty. Wherever Act 29 provided
the death sentence, the punishment is now life imprisonment.

The punishment for murder in Ghana is therefore life imprisonment, not death. A
court can no longer sentence a person to death for murder.

The change covers ordinary crimes that previously carried the death penalty,
including murder, attempted murder, genocide, piracy and smuggling of gold or
diamonds. Older copies of the Criminal Offences Act still show "sentenced to death";
that text has been replaced.

The Armed Forces (Amendment) Act, 2023 made the same change for military offences.

Prisoners who were on death row have their sentences replaced by life imprisonment.

Reports at the time noted that the death penalty for high treason is written into
the Constitution and was not removed by this Act, because changing that part of the
Constitution needs a separate process.
