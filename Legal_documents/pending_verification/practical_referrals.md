Statutes say what the law is. This file says where a person actually goes, which is
what most people asking the bot need next.

## Rent Control Department (landlord and tenant disputes)

Handles: excessive rent advance, unlawful rent increases, illegal eviction,
failure to maintain premises, withheld deposits, harassment by a landlord.
Established under the Rent Act, 1963 (Act 220); sits under the Ministry of Works,
Housing and Water Resources.

- Head office: Behind Ghana Health Service Head Office, Dodoo Street, Accra
- Postal: P.O. Box 1590, Accra
- Phone: 0302 670 406 · 0302 670 399 · 0278 331 950
- Online: rentcontrol.mwh.gov.gh — register a tenancy, file a complaint, track a case
- Reported coverage: 15 digitised offices across 11 regions

How a complaint is filed: log in to the portal, open the Complaints section, and
complete the online form (the equivalent of Form 7) with the property details, the
names and contact details of both parties, and a description of the problem.
Someone without internet access can go to the Accra office or a regional office.

## Legal Aid Commission (free legal representation and advice)

Handles: criminal defence, and civil matters for people who cannot afford a lawyer.

- Head office: Former Prosecution Division Building, OAGMoJ, Starlets 91 Road, Accra
- Phone: 0302 975 749
- Email: info@lac.gov.gh
- Greater Accra Regional Office: Council for Law Reporting Building, Ministries
  Enclave, Liberia Road, opposite Ministries Police Station
- Greater Accra phone: 0302 975 922 · gar@lac.gov.gh
